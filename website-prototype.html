How we work

The brief is a question,
not a specification.

Most production companies start from logistics: venue, catering, AV, run-of-show. RUNO starts from a different place. Before anything is specified, we ask what this event should do to the people in the room — not during the evening, but in the weeks and months after it.

This is the logic of cultural programming applied to commercial briefs. It comes from fifteen years of producing festivals, operas, and diplomatic events — forms where the narrative structure is non-negotiable and the production serves it. When that discipline is applied to a brand gala or a corporate offsite, the result is not an "elevated event." It is an experience with a specific intention, produced to deliver it.

We take a small number of projects each year. This is not a capacity constraint; it is a quality constraint. Every project receives the full attention of the founding team from brief to close.

FORGE

In-house production workshop

We build what
we can't subcontract.

The centrepiece table for the Bentley Tbilisi Gala Dinner was oak and steel, 3.4 metres, singular. It was designed as part of the concept, not selected from a catalogue. That specificity was not available at any price elsewhere — because the object didn't exist until we made it.

FORGE is RUNO's in-house fabrication workshop. It exists because the productions we make require physical objects with a level of specificity that the events industry cannot supply.

Custom tables. Structural installations. Set elements. Objects that are designed for a particular brief, built from particular materials, intended to do a particular thing in a particular space. Not sourced. Not rented. Built.

When a client asks how we guarantee the quality of the physical production, the answer is direct: we made it. FORGE is not a vendor relationship. It is the workshop two floors below the studio.

FORGE also accepts direct commissions — furniture, interiors, objects — independent of event production. Enquiries via the studio.

Four days in Georgia built around six creative disciplines — each running as a short residency with a practitioner based here: a sound artist, a choreographer, a choral director, a ceramicist, a filmmaker, a writer.

Not a team-building programme. A collective experience that produces something that didn't exist before the group arrived — a recording, a ceramic object, a text. The work is real. The output leaves with the team.

The format was developed for HR and People Experience leaders at distributed companies who have run the standard formats and want to know what comes after the wine-tasting and the workshop. It works because it asks something genuine of the group — and Georgia, as a place, makes genuine things possible.

Available from April through October. We produce a small number of Offsite programmes each season alongside the main production slate.

Five thousand years of culture at a single crossing point — Colchis, the Silk Road, Persian, Russian, Soviet, European — without resolving into any one of them. This is not an exotic backdrop. RUNO works from Tbilisi because this is where we are from. We know which venues carry weight and which ones are merely available. We know the producers, the craftspeople, the artists — which ones are exceptional and which ones cannot be found through a directory. We know the city before the brief arrives.

For international clients who come to Tbilisi: we are not a local contact. We are the reason to come.

For clients based in Georgia: RUNO brings a production methodology and an international network developed across fifteen years of working at scale in Russia, Europe, and the Gulf.
