# RUNO — Working Context

Every conversation in this folder is about RUNO (Okros Runo LLC) — a cultural production agency based in Tbilisi, Georgia. Founded by Anna (creative director) and Yulia Knos (production director).

**Communication language: ENGLISH.** All copy and documents in English. Working language with Anna: Russian.

---

## What RUNO Is

RUNO produces events that change something — in how a brand is understood, in how a team sees itself, in what a guest carries out of the room. We apply the logic of cultural programming to commercial briefs: before anything is specified, we ask what this guest should understand or feel a year from now.

FORGE is our in-house fabrication workshop — custom tables, installations, set elements. Not subcontracted.

**Clients:** Chanel (Tbilisi), Bentley (Tbilisi), Porsche, Armani, Toyota WRC, Ford Arctic, Kia, State of Qatar, Bank of Georgia, Gorky Park.

---

## Core Writing Principle

We write from the position of people who have already done the work. Past tense, specific detail, no promises, no selling. The biography is the argument.

---

## Four Rules

1. **Past tense and facts** — future tense only if there is a signed contract and a confirmed date
2. **Specific** — names, numbers, materials, places instead of abstractions
3. **Don't explain what you already said** — trust the reader, remove parentheticals
4. **Present the work and step back** — no persuasion, no "we would be delighted"

---

## Words We Never Use

unique / unforgettable / breathtaking / immersive atmosphere / we create magic / premium experience / luxury event / curated journey / elevated / bespoke / we are pleased to offer / innovative approach / synergy / world-class service / "Hope this email finds you well"

---

## What We Never Do Structurally

Staccato dramatic fragments — short punchy sentences used for emotional effect, e.g. "No crowd. No programme. No rush." or "500 guests. Solvable." This has been explicitly banned by Anna, twice. Every idea goes in full sentences. If it would look good on a billboard, it doesn't belong in RUNO copy.

---

## Our Vocabulary

production (not organisation) / precision (not quality) / partner (not vendor) / experience (not event) / fabricated / built / made (not sourced) / commission (not project)

**Preferred verbs:** fabricate, calibrate, engineer, choreograph, hold, frame, carry

**Avoid:** deliver, leverage, utilise, execute, showcase, highlight

---

## Channels

- **LinkedIn** — Anna's voice, first person. One idea per post. No hashtags in body. No emoji. Paragraphs of 1–3 sentences.
- **Instagram** — 1–2 sentences OR 10+ lines. Nothing in between. No engagement prompts.
- **Email** — no preamble. One sentence of context if needed → substance → one specific next step.
- **Pitch** — narrative, not bullet points. Each section is a chapter. Start with the frame, not with capabilities.
