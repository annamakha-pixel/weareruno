What FORGE Is

Premium
Production
Studio

FORGE is RUNO's in-house fabrication and design studio in Tbilisi — a 300 m² facility where set design, structural engineering, lighting integration, and physical fabrication happen under one roof.

We unite scenographers, lighting designers, structural engineers, and fabricators who treat production as architecture, not decoration. Every line carries intent. Every material is chosen, not sourced. The result is environments that shape how people move and what they remember — not spaces that simply fill volume.

FORGE builds for events, cultural institutions, retail, theatre, urban installations, and corporate productions. The work does not get subcontracted. It gets built.

The Facility
