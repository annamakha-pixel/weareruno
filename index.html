Who We Are

We
Know
The Way.

RUNO is a cultural production studio based in Tbilisi. We apply the logic of cultural programming to commercial briefs — before anything is specified, we ask what the guest should understand or feel a year from now.

In ancient mythology, the Argonauts sailed to Colchis seeking the Golden Fleece — "Runō." They came for treasure but discovered something greater: the transformative power that emerges where different worlds converge. This land, where continents meet, has always been a place of intersection.

We are based in Tbilisi and work worldwide.

The People

The Team

RUNO operates on a permanent core and a wide network of specialists assembled per commission — sound and lighting engineers, scenographers, translators, protocol officers, caterers, PR managers, and venue coordinators across Georgia and Europe.

The core team has worked together since 2018. The extended network has been built over twenty years of commissions from major opera houses to the Arctic.
