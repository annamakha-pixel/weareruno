RUNO is a cultural production studio in Tbilisi. We apply the logic of cultural programming to commercial briefs: before anything is specified, we ask what the guest should understand or feel a year from now.

Work

Official reception for the State of Qatar in St. Petersburg. 500 guests - diplomats, royal advisors, and cultural figures from more than 30 countries. Catering by the Emir's personal chef. UN-level security protocol, precise seating by diplomatic rank, arrival sequence coordinated to the minute.

Client: Qatar Embassy, QRCC · St. Petersburg, Russia · 2021

Global campaign launch bringing over 200 international guests to Tbilisi. Full production for a fragrance launch in collaboration with DAZZZLING and Chanel CIS. The city served as the backdrop - not as a theme, but as a genuine frame for the collection's character.

Client: DAZZZLING, Chanel CIS · 200+ international guests · Tbilisi, Georgia · 2025

The first shopping festival in Tbilisi, held over two weekends at Tbilisi Outlet Village. RUNO developed the concept and produced the festival end to end: concerts, public talks, a beach zone, brand activations, and a programme built on pre-Soviet Georgian carnival traditions.

Client: Tbilisi Outlet Village · Tbilisi, Georgia · 2026

RUNO is the production team behind Loco Park, the revitalization of a former factory into a new zone for recreation, culture, and creative life.

Client: Loco Alliance · Tbilisi, Georgia · 2025-2026

Full concert production for Hurts' Tbilisi debut - venue management, technical execution, and coordination with the global tour production for an audience of over 2,000. The first time the band played Georgia; the production was calibrated to treat that fact as significant.

Client: HONEYCOMB Global · 2,000+ audience · Tbilisi, Georgia · 2023

Moscow's iconic public space transformed into a hub of creativity. Nine days of festival. International headliners. Over one million visitors. The event honoured the park's history while looking ahead to its future - combining culture, innovation, and community engagement at a scale that required a different kind of precision than any single-venue production.

1,024,000+ visitors · 9 days · International headliners · Moscow, Russia · 2018

A contemporary opera festival that redefined the boundaries of classical art. Experimental storytelling, cutting-edge production, and collaborations with world-leading theatres. A platform for new voices and bold ideas in opera, running over four years.

Moscow, Russia · 2011–2015

End-to-end production at the Stanislavsky Theatre. Director: Vasily Barkhatov. Starring: Renata Litvinova. Orchestra, choir, and ballet - five cars presented on stage. A theatrical launch at the intersection of automotive and performing arts that treated the car not as a product to be displayed, but as a character in a production.

Client: KIA Motors, PRT · Moscow, Russia · 2013
