Kultpokhod

Programme developed for HRMoscow · 2015-2016

A cultural programme built for HR departments, based on our own research into how a company's mission and business goals can be revealed through art.

Each programme started from the client's goals, not from a ticket. We chose the performance, exhibition or concert that spoke to what the company was trying to say to its people, and built the evening around it.

  • KultpokhodPrivate visits to theatres, museums and art spaces, matched to the company's goals: a look behind the scenes with the people who make the work.
  • Hands-onWorkshops and joint work with artists, from sitting in on an orchestra rehearsal to making something together.
  • Corporate lecture seriesTalks with directors, musicians, curators and critics on how to read contemporary culture.
