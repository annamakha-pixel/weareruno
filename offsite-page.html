Corporate Offsite in Georgia

A team that works apart rarely makes anything together. On the offsite each group spends its afternoons with a Tbilisi practitioner and leaves with something made: a recording, a route, an object built in our workshop.

What's included

RUNO takes care of every part of the offsite, from start to finish.

01

Arrival

A welcome desk at the airport and transfers in both directions. Keys and programme booklets are handed out before the team reaches the hotel.

02

Working sessions

A room set up for the team, with AV, translation, fast connectivity and materials.

03

Practices

Art and sport sessions of three to four hours, each led by a Tbilisi practitioner and each ending with something made.

04

Food

Dietary profiles are collected in advance, and every meal is planned around them.

05

Evenings

Live music, themed dinners and wine, and on the last night a supra with a tamada.

06*

Merch and decor

Branded materials and keepsakes, made in FORGE, our workshop in Tbilisi.

Practices

There are twelve disciplines in two tracks, and each group chooses its own.

Art TrackSound · Move · Sing · Build · See · Write

Sound

Modular synthesizers, loops and textures. The session ends with a recorded composition.

Move

Contemporary dance with a choreographer, about how bodies communicate when they move together.

Sing

Georgian polyphonic choir, where each person holds a part while the others hold theirs.

Build

Sculpture and object-making in FORGE. Every participant takes something home.

See

Photography and film: an assignment, a shoot and an edit.

Write

Poetry or free writing with a Georgian writer, to a set task and time limit, then read aloud.

Sport TrackYoga · Run · Climb · Horse · Bike · Row

Yoga

Adapted for all levels, outdoors when the weather allows.

Run

A guided run through the venue grounds and the countryside around it.

Climb

Bouldering or rope climbing, with the rest of the group watching from below.

Horse

Riding with local horsemen, for complete beginners as well.

Bike

A guided cycling route through the countryside, chosen for the views, not the difficulty.

Row

Rowing for technique, rhythm and synchrony, at venues with a lake or river.

Questions

These are the questions teams ask most often before booking.

Our team is remote and barely knows each other.

That is the situation the programme is built for. When Microsoft studied 61,182 employees during remote work, the ties between teams weakened and remote tools did not bring them back. The programme is built to restore those ties through work done side by side.

Microsoft / Nature Human Behaviour, 2021

Our engineers won't do a singing session.

They can choose the sport track: yoga, running, climbing, horses, cycling or rowing, all suitable for beginners. The art sessions don't require talent either, because each one is a task with a clear limit, and the limit does most of the work.

Who runs it?

One RUNO producer, from the first call to the debrief after departure. Venues, transfers, food, practitioners, evenings and materials all go through one contact.
