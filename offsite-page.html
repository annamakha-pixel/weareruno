Why it works

People bond
around what
they built together.

When people create something together, the brain encodes it differently — oxytocin is released, a shared social history forms. Co-creation activates the same neural reward circuits as food and shelter. Belonging is not a mood — it is a biological state.

Microsoft tracked 61,182 employees across six months of remote work and found that collaboration networks became siloed — the weak ties between teams that generate trust and new ideas simply collapsed. Remote tools did not rebuild them.

RUNO builds the programme around doing. The belonging forms around what the team made together — the recording, the route, the object someone kept. That shared history exists independently of the company. It is what holds.

Questions

What the
evidence says.

Does a few days together actually change how a team works?

Yes — under specific conditions. BetterUp's study of 1,789 US workers found a 56% improvement in job performance and 50% reduction in turnover risk in teams with high belonging. Gallup reports that engaged teams are 21% more profitable than disengaged ones. What distinguishes programmes that work from those that don't: shared physical experience, co-creation, and sufficient context shift. A conference room doesn't provide these. A week in Georgia does.

Daniel Coyle spent years studying the world's highest-performing teams and found that belonging cues — physical proximity, shared effort, turn-taking under pressure — are the actual mechanism. Not talks about values.

BetterUp, "The Value of Belonging at Work," 2019 · Gallup, State of the Global Workplace, 2023 · Daniel Coyle, The Culture Code, 2018

Why physical and creative activity — not workshops and keynotes?

Because the brain responds differently to physical co-creation than to passive information. Dr. Matthew Lieberman (UCLA) has shown that social connection activates the same dopaminergic reward circuits as food and money — it is a primary drive. When people create together, oxytocin — the neurochemical associated with trust — is released. A recording made together, a route run together, an object built together: these are encoded as episodic memory with emotional salience. That is what stays past the second Monday.

A keynote is forgotten within a week. The choir practice is not.

"Being socially connected is our brain's lifelong passion."
— Matthew Lieberman, UCLA

Lieberman, Social: Why Our Brains Are Wired to Connect, Crown, 2013 · NIH, "Oxytocin as the Neurobiological Basis of Synchronization," 2021

Our team is remote. They barely know each other.

That is exactly the condition this programme is designed for. Microsoft tracked 61,182 employees over six months of remote work and found that collaboration networks became more siloed and static — the "weak ties" between teams that generate new ideas and mutual trust collapsed. No remote tool rebuilt them. Physical presence did.

The more distributed the team, the more deliberate the investment in belonging needs to be. A week in Georgia does more for social capital in five days than six months of weekly standups.

Microsoft / Nature Human Behaviour, "The effects of remote work on collaboration among information workers," 2021 · Holtz, Jaffe et al.

How do I justify this budget to our CFO?

50 people at $900 = $45,000. SHRM benchmarks the cost of replacing one mid-level employee at 125–150% of annual salary — before accounting for onboarding lag, lost institutional knowledge, and team disruption. At a $120,000 average salary, that is $150,000–$180,000 per departure.

If the offsite prevents one departure, it has covered itself. If it prevents three, it has returned the investment four times. Gallup estimates that actively disengaged employees cost organisations 18% of their annual salary in lost productivity — at 50 people, that is nine full-time equivalents producing below capacity. The question is not whether to invest in retention. It is whether to do it deliberately.

SHRM Employee Turnover Benchmark · Gallup, State of the Global Workplace, 2023

Our engineers won't do a singing session.

The sport track exists for this. Six disciplines — yoga, running, climbing, horseback riding, cycling, rowing — all designed for zero prior experience. Groups choose their track at the start of the programme. Some mix both across sessions.

The art track is not about talent. Georgian polyphonic choir does not require you to be a singer. Modular synthesis does not require you to be a musician. The task in every session is to produce something together under a specific constraint. The constraint is what does the work, not the medium.

Amy Edmondson, Harvard Business School: psychological safety — not comfort — is what allows people to take creative risk.
