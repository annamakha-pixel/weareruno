The Opportunity

The Market
Is Moving
Toward Us

The corporate offsite market is large and growing. The specific segment RUNO addresses — premium offsites for distributed technology and product companies — is the fastest-growing part of it.

Since 2022, the concentration of distributed teams has accelerated sharply. The companies most likely to need a RUNO offsite are those whose HR directors are already spending significant budget on inferior alternatives.

The gap is not in demand. The gap is in supply of a product that actually delivers what it promises: measurable change in team cohesion, built into the programme design.
